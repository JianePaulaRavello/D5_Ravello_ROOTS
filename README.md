# ROOTS – Explore your ROOTS

**Jiane-Paula Ravello** · Cross Media Design I · D-5 final package

ROOTS is a festival for children of immigrants and their families. It takes place on Sat 23 January 2027 at Brussels Expo.
The full report is in `D5_ROOTS.pdf`.

## 1_Touchpoint

The core touchpoint is a clickable Instagram Story prototype made in Figma. It has no code, so there is nothing to install or run: open the link and tap through the frames.

## 3_Motion

The motion principle is a luggage tag that is pulled off a suitcase and swings, after which the word is written. It is used in the launch story and as a loop on a screen at the sticker desk.
